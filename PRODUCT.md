# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Learners and developers exploring HowzatLang through a browser-based compiler playground. They write match programs, learn how source moves through compiler stages, and inspect the resulting cricket simulation.

## Product Purpose

HowzatLang is an educational cricket-themed domain-specific language and compiler. The studio helps users author and compile match descriptions, understand the compiler's transformations, and review the scorecard and delivery-by-delivery simulation. Success means these three activities feel connected and the generated output is easy to inspect.

## Brand Personality

Clear, precise, and approachable, with a light cricket character that supports learning without distracting from the work.

## Anti-references

Avoid cramped workspaces, green-dominant styling, decorative sports imagery, and visual patterns that obscure the source, compiler stages, or match data.

## Design Principles

- Keep authoring, compiling, and inspecting output in one coherent workflow.
- Make compiler stages and generated representations easy to find and understand.
- Present cricket simulation data clearly and preserve the limits of the prototype.
- Use familiar workbench patterns so the interface stays predictable during focused tasks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, responsive layouts, and respect reduced-motion preferences. Keep critical status information understandable without relying on color alone.
